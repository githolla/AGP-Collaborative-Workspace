One project, three surfaces

Kantata stays the system of record — nothing that works there gets rebuilt. The two new surfaces exist for the two things Kantata can't do today: managing project changes automatically (Kelly does this manually) and collaborating per client in one place (instead of scattered Teams chats). Nobody has a new daily login: you go in when a Teams or email notification pulls you in, fix the thing, and it flows back to Kantata.
System of record

Kantata

Where projects, tasks, hours, and budgets live. Unchanged.

  • Projects created here (or promoted from the sandbox)
  • Time entries, milestones, assignments
  • Both new surfaces read from it — and write approved changes back
The hero — Kara's ask

Collaboration Workspace

One standing room per client: the team, the client, and contractors around one plan — instead of 100 group chats.

  • Kara's wireframe: Home · Plan · Client Dashboard · Files · Discussions · Access
  • Live Kantata delivery: milestones, tasks, team, hours pulse
  • @mentions notify; AI drafts the weekly client update for approval
  • Client-safe by rule — internal numbers never render for guests
The fixer — Kelly's ask

Resource & Task Allocation

Manages project changes: when something drifts, AI proposes the fix — hours, people, budget impact — in one place instead of six manual steps.

  • Watches every project against its parameters
  • Alert → suggested fix → people ranked by fit & availability
  • Budget impact shown as hours move
  • Approve once → writes back to Kantata

A day in the life — the test project

1KantataKelly's test project exists in Kantata with its plan, budget, and team. Both surfaces already see it live.
2Real worldSomething moves — the client holds for two weeks, print production slips, a task runs long.
3Teams / emailResource & Task detects the drift and pings only the people who need to act (each person picks Teams or email). No daily check-ins, no 5 pm surprises.
4Resource & TaskKelly clicks in. "What needs you" shows the issue, the AI's suggested fix, hours re-spread across the timeline, and the budget impact of the move.
5Resource & TaskOne of six roles needs a person — the AI ranks the top two by fit, shows who's free, one click assigns.
6CollaborationA judgment call comes up — "can you take this? you're free but marked OOO" — that conversation happens in the client's workspace, on the record, @mention notifies. The milestone shift is already visible on the client dashboard.
7KantataKelly approves → the change writes back to Kantata: tasks, hours, assignments stay accurate. (Write-back is staged behind approval; lands with the Azure deploy.)
8CollaborationFriday: the AI drafts the weekly client update from real campaign, milestone, and task state — the account lead edits and posts. The client never sees internal numbers.
Who logs in, when: nobody by default.
